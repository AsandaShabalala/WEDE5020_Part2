Welcome to AFTO

Best Old Age Home in the Country

Helping Our Elderly Live With Dignity

At AFTO, we provide a safe and caring environment where elderly people can feel at home, stay active and enjoy a fulfilling life.

Association For The Old AFTO was founded with a simple mission: to give elderly members of our community a safe, warm, and caring place to call home. Over the years we have grown from a small family-run house into a trusted care facility. At AFTO we believe elderly people should also have a community, whether it is sports, movies, painting etc at AFTO elderly people can have access to such communities and be taken of care by caring Volunteers and our trained professionals
